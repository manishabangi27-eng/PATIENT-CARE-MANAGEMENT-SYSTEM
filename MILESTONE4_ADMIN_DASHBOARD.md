# Milestone 4 – Complete Admin Dashboard

The administrator dashboard is the central Milestone 4 control center.

## Dashboard Cards
- Total registered patients
- Active/registered doctors
- Today's appointments
- Completed consultations
- Pending laboratory reports
- Total bills generated
- Unread notifications
- Patient satisfaction score

## Analytics Charts
- 30-day appointment trends
- Monthly patient registrations (6 months)
- Doctor-wise consultation count
- Patient demographics
- Disease/diagnosis distribution
- Laboratory test statistics
- Monthly paid-revenue analysis

## Financial Monitoring
- Paid revenue
- Unpaid/outstanding revenue
- Billing quick action

## Operations
- Recent appointments with accept/cancel actions
- Recent patient feedback
- Average satisfaction rating
- Recent system activity/login logs

## Quick Actions
- Administrative reports
- Feedback and satisfaction
- Manage doctors
- Manage nurses
- View patients
- Manage appointments
- Billing
- Notifications

## Milestone 4 Alignment
This dashboard implements the dashboard cards and charts described in the supplied Milestone 4 requirements, including patient/doctor statistics, appointments, consultations, laboratory reports, bills, revenue, notifications, registrations, appointment trends, disease distribution, laboratory statistics, doctor-wise consultation counts and satisfaction metrics.
